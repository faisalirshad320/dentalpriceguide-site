What does dental work cost in the US?

Plain-English price guides for 16 common dental procedures, with every figure taken from published claims data and national cost surveys, and named on the page. Medically reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS.

Quick answer

Most routine dental work costs between about $85 and $160 for a cleaning and $1,100 to $2,000 for a crown, while a single dental implant runs $2,800 to $5,600 without insurance (Delta Dental claims data). Insurance usually pays 100% of preventive care, 80% of basic work and 50% of major work, up to an annual maximum.

Where our prices come from

We use out-of-network claims data published by Delta Dental, the Synchrony / CareCredit Average Procedural Cost Study (2023–2024), GoodRx and dental-fee data based on the ADA Survey of Dental Fees. When two sources disagree we show both and explain why. Read the full methodology.
