How Much Do Braces Cost for Adults?

Medically reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBSUpdated October 30, 20265 price sources
Quick answer

Braces for adults cost about $6,343 on average for metal braces, $5,834 for ceramic braces, $9,221 for lingual braces and $5,108 for clear aligners, according to CareCredit. Adults pay similar fees to teens for the same treatment, but adult dental plans are less likely to include orthodontic coverage.

Key takeaways
  • Adult braces average $6,343 (metal), $5,834 (ceramic), $9,221 (lingual) and $5,108 (aligners) per CareCredit.
  • Pearl lists comprehensive orthodontic treatment at $3,680 to $9,650 nationally.
  • Adults often choose less visible options such as ceramic, lingual or aligners.
  • Adult dental plans frequently exclude orthodontics or have low lifetime maximums.
  • Payment plans, HSA or FSA funds and dental schools can make treatment affordable.

How much do braces cost for adults?

Braces for adults cost about the same as braces for teenagers for the same treatment, because orthodontic fees are based on the complexity of the case rather than the patient's age. CareCredit's averages are $6,343 for metal braces, $5,834 for ceramic, $9,221 for lingual and $5,108 for clear aligners.

Pearl's dental cost data lists comprehensive orthodontic treatment (CDT D8080) at $3,680 to $9,650 nationally, with an average of $5,645. Delta Dental cites $3,000 to $7,000 for traditional metal braces and $8,000 to $10,000 for lingual braces. Your quote depends on your location, how much correction you need and how long treatment will take.

Why do adults get braces?

Adults get braces for many reasons: teeth that were never straightened, teeth that shifted after childhood braces because retainers were not worn, crowding that has worsened with age, or bite problems causing wear, jaw discomfort or difficulty cleaning.

Straightening can make teeth easier to clean, reduce uneven wear and sometimes prepare the mouth for other dental work such as implants, crowns or bridges. Many adults also want the confidence of a straighter smile. There is no upper age limit for braces as long as teeth and gums are healthy.

What braces options are best for adults?

Adults often prefer braces that are less noticeable, especially for work and social life. Each option has trade-offs in cost, visibility and convenience.

  • Metal braces: most versatile and usually the most affordable fixed option, but visible.
  • Ceramic braces: tooth-coloured brackets that blend in; slightly more fragile.
  • Lingual braces: hidden behind the teeth; the most expensive and can affect speech at first.
  • Clear aligners: removable, nearly invisible trays; only work if worn most of the day.

Is orthodontic treatment different for adults?

Orthodontic treatment for adults works the same way as for teenagers, but a few factors differ. Adult bone is denser, so teeth may move a little more slowly. Adults are more likely to have gum disease, missing teeth, crowns or fillings that the orthodontist must plan around.

Before braces, an adult may need a dental check, cleaning and treatment of any cavities or gum problems. Some adults with significant jaw size differences benefit from combining braces with jaw surgery. Your orthodontist will explain whether anything in your situation adds time or cost.

Does insurance cover adult braces?

Insurance covers adult braces less often than children's braces. Many dental plans limit orthodontic benefits to children under a certain age, and some exclude orthodontics entirely. Plans that do include adult orthodontics usually have a separate lifetime maximum for orthodontic care.

Delta Dental's coverage guide explains how deductibles, coinsurance and maximums work. Some employers offer plans with adult orthodontic coverage, so check during open enrolment if you are planning treatment.

How can adults pay for braces?

Adults can make braces more affordable with several common payment options.

  • In-house payment plans: many orthodontists spread the fee over the treatment period, often with a down payment.
  • HSA or FSA funds: orthodontic treatment is usually an eligible expense, paid with pre-tax money.
  • Medical credit or financing: check interest terms carefully.
  • Dental school clinics: supervised orthodontic residents may charge less.
  • Comparing quotes: fees vary between practices for the same plan.

How long do adults wear braces?

Adults typically wear braces for about one to three years, depending on the problem being corrected. Mild crowding may finish sooner, while bite corrections or complex cases take longer. Clear aligners can be similar in length for suitable cases when worn as directed.

After treatment, adults wear retainers to hold the result, often at night long term. Retainers are especially important for adults who already experienced teeth shifting after earlier treatment.

Are braces worth it as an adult?

Braces are worth it for many adults, both for appearance and for long-term dental health. Straighter teeth can be easier to clean and less prone to uneven wear. The investment is significant, so it helps to understand the full cost, your options and how long the result will last with retainer wear.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends first orthodontic checks for children by age 7, as noted in Delta Dental's parent guide, but it is never too late for an evaluation. Healthline's braces cost guide offers further background. If you choose metal braces, you can still personalise the look with braces colors.

What is included in the price of adult braces?

The price of adult braces usually covers the whole course of active treatment, but what is included varies by practice. Most quotes include the bonding appointment, the brackets and wires, regular adjustment visits and the removal appointment.

Items that are sometimes extra include the initial records (X-rays, photos and scans), emergency visits for broken brackets, replacement aligners if trays are lost, and retainers after treatment. Retainers matter a great deal for adults, so ask whether the first set is included and what replacements cost.

If you need other dental work before braces, such as a cleaning, fillings or gum treatment, that is billed by your general dentist. In some adult cases, teeth may need to be extracted to make room, or implants planned after alignment. Ask the orthodontist to list every step so you can budget for the full journey, not just the braces fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much do braces cost for adults?

Braces for adults cost about $6,343 for metal braces, $5,834 for ceramic braces, $9,221 for lingual braces and $5,108 for clear aligners on average, according to CareCredit. Pearl lists comprehensive treatment at $3,680 to $9,650 nationally.

Are braces more expensive for adults than kids?

Braces are generally not more expensive for adults than kids for the same treatment, because fees depend on complexity. Adults may pay more out of pocket because adult dental plans are less likely to include orthodontic coverage.

What is the cheapest type of braces for adults?

Metal braces and clear aligners are often among the more affordable options for adults. CareCredit's averages are $6,343 for metal braces and $5,108 for clear aligners, though the price for any individual depends on the case.

Does insurance cover braces for adults?

Some dental insurance plans cover adult braces, usually up to a lifetime orthodontic maximum, but many limit orthodontic benefits to children or exclude them. Check your plan or ask your employer about plans with adult orthodontic coverage.

Can you get braces at 40 or 50?

You can get braces at 40, 50 or older as long as your teeth and gums are healthy. Adult treatment may take a little longer, and gum disease or missing teeth must be addressed first. Many adults choose ceramic braces or clear aligners.
