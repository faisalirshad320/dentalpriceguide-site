Editorial policy

Research

Each page starts from a fixed set of sourced price facts. Writers may use only those figures. Clinical guidance follows mainstream sources such as the American Dental Association, specialty associations and major academic medical centers.

Writing

Pages answer the reader's question in the first sentence, use plain English, and say when something varies or is uncertain. We use AI-assisted drafting tools under these rules, and every page is checked against its source list before publishing.

Review

Pages are medically reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS. The review date appears at the top of each page.

Corrections

If you find an error, tell us. We correct factual errors promptly and update the page date.

Independence

We do not accept payment for coverage. Ads are served by Google AdSense and are labelled as ads by Google.
