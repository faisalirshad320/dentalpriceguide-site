How we price dental procedures

Every dollar figure on Dental Price Guide comes from a published, named source. We never estimate a price ourselves, and each page lists its sources at the bottom.

Our sources

SourceWhat it measures
Delta Dental cost guidesAverage out-of-network charges from Delta Dental's internal claims data (2021–2025, year stated on each page).
CareCredit dentist price listSynchrony Average Procedural Cost Study, ASQ360° Market Research, 2023–2024, all 50 states and DC.
GoodRx procedure guidesDentist-reviewed cost guides; used for denture types not covered elsewhere.
Pearl dental cost dataPopulation-weighted national averages based on ADA fee survey data (May 2026).
HealthlineDentist-reviewed articles; used for bonding and braces ranges.

Why two numbers sometimes differ

Delta Dental reports what out-of-network dentists charged on real claims. The CareCredit study surveys practice prices. The two measure different things, so their averages can differ by a few hundred dollars. When they differ, we show both and say which is which.

What our prices do not include

National averages do not reflect your city, your dentist, your insurance plan or the complexity of your case. A root canal price, for example, usually does not include the crown that follows. Each page says what is and is not included where the source makes it clear.

How often we check

Prices were last checked in October 2026. We review sources at least twice a year and update a page's date when its figures change.
