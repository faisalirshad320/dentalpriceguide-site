Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or health information through the site. Our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, time) for security and troubleshooting.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

Your rights

Residents of California, the EU and the UK have rights over personal data, including access and deletion. Because we do not hold personal data beyond server logs and ad cookies, most requests are handled through Google's controls above. For anything else, contact us.

Children

This site is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
